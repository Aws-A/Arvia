The Arvia Consultation

Thank you for your interest in Arvia Interiors. We look forward to connecting with you to discuss your project and design vision. Our Director of Operations, Eva Mazinani, is available to learn more about your space, understand your goals, and answer any questions regarding our design services and process. This initial consultation allows us to align on scope, style, and expectations, establishing a clear and thoughtful foundation for your project before moving into the design phase.

Residential Design Consultation

A focused design consultation tailored to your home, lifestyle, and project vision. Led by Eva Mazinani, an internationally experienced designer, this consultation is an in-depth conversation where we explore your space, understand your goals, and define a clear design direction for your interior. It is the foundation for creating a cohesive, functional, and elevated environment tailored to your lifestyle. What we discuss: • Full-scope project goals and overall design direction • Space planning and furniture arrangement consulting • Kitchen, renovation, and construction possibilities • Paint colours, materials, and finish direction • Lighting atmosphere and spatial flow • Window treatment options • Creative statement walls, focal points, and custom features • Built-ins, proportions, scale, and balance • Rug selection, flooring direction, and dimensions • Accessories, styling, and staging guidance • General renovation recommendations and trusted referrals • Clear next steps for the design phase This consultation is designed to bring clarity, confidence, and creative direction to your project before moving into the full design process. Details: Duration: 1 hour Format: In-home or virtual Fee: Project-based consultation rate
