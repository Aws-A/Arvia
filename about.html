About Us

A luxury residential design studio specializing in kitchens, renovations, and material-driven interiors throughout Toronto and the GTA. Rooted in a client-focused and design-led approach, Arvia Design transforms ideas into refined spatial experiences that balance function, proportion, and timeless aesthetics. Every project begins with understanding how you live and evolves into a carefully considered interior defined by clarity, character, and intention. With over 12 years of international design experience and 2 years in Canada, alongside a background in Industrial Design, Eva Mazinani brings a layered understanding of space, material, and detail. Her work blends structural thinking with aesthetic refinement, resulting in interiors that feel both composed and effortless. The studio focuses on custom kitchens, full-home renovations, bespoke millwork, and cohesive interiors shaped by natural and architectural finishes such as lime wash, Venetian plaster, and microcement. These materials are selected not as trends, but as tools to create depth, warmth, and quiet sophistication. Arvia Design remains closely involved in every stage of the process—from concept development and design direction to material selection and visual refinement—ensuring a highly considered and cohesive outcome from start to finish.

Our Approach

We transform ideas into thoughtfully designed interiors that reflect each client's lifestyle, vision, and individuality.

We create functional, well-crafted spaces that balance beauty, comfort, and practicality while maximizing the potential of every project.
